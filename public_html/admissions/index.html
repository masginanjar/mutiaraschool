Admissions

Enrolment for the 2027/2028 school year is open for preschool, primary and junior high. Transfer students are welcome.

How to enrol

  1. 1

    Register online

    Fill in the enrolment form. You may also visit the school and our team will help you complete it.

  2. 2

    Pay the registration fee

    The registration fee covers selection and observation.

  3. 3

    Complete your data

    Upload your child’s details and documents online.

  4. 4

    Observation and interview

    Held at the school: one day for preschool and primary, two days for junior high. Results are announced 2–3 days later.

Entry requirements

Preschool

Age 3 for Playgroup, 4 for Kindergarten A and 5 for Kindergarten B, by July of the school year.

Primary

Age 6 by July of the school year, and in good physical and mental health.

Junior high

Completed primary school (SD/MI), holds an NISN, and passes the academic test and interview.

Documents to prepare

  • One 3×4 photo with a red background
  • A copy of the birth certificate
  • A copy of the family card (Kartu Keluarga)
  • A copy of a parent’s or guardian’s ID card (KTP)
  • Junior high only: legalised copies of grade 4–6 reports and a doctor’s health certificate

Common questions

How much are the fees?

Fees differ by level and intake period, so we do not list them here. Message us on WhatsApp and our team will send the current fee sheet.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

Yes. Instalments are possible; parents sign a commitment form with the finance office.

Do you accept transfer students?

Yes, from any school, as long as the administrative requirements are met.

What if we miss the interview date?

The application is moved to the next intake period.

Can I register for a later school year?

Yes. Register through the form, then confirm with us on WhatsApp.
