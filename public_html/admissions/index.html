Entry requirements

Playgroup (KB)
Age 3 by July of the school year
Kindergarten A
Age 4 by July of the school year
Kindergarten B
Age 5 by July of the school year
Primary grade 1
Age 6. Under 6 with a psychologist’s recommendation letter
Junior high
Completed primary school

Documents to prepare

  • One 3×4 photo with a red background
  • A copy of the birth certificate
  • A copy of the family card (Kartu Keluarga)
  • A copy of a parent’s or guardian’s ID card (KTP)
  • Junior high only: legalised copies of grade 4–6 reports and a doctor’s health certificate

Common questions

How much are the fees?

Fees differ by level and intake period, so we do not list them here. Message us on WhatsApp and our team will send the current fee sheet.

Can fees be paid in instalments?

Yes. Parents sign a commitment form with the finance office.

Do you accept transfer students?

Yes, from any school, as long as the administrative requirements are met.

Can I register offline?

Yes. Visit the school and our staff will help you register online.
