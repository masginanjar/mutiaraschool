Facilities

A modern, child-friendly campus near Udayana University, designed so children feel safe, comfortable and free to explore.

Spaces for learning

Classrooms are air-conditioned and supported by a library, a computer and e-learning lab, a language lab, a multimedia room and a music room. Wi-Fi is available across the learning areas.

What you will find on campus

Learning

Learning centre, air-conditioned classrooms, library, computer and e-learning lab, language lab, multimedia room.

Arts and worship

Music room, prayer room and a dedicated tahfidz room.

Sport and play

Sport centre and sports field, indoor and outdoor playgrounds.

Care and wellbeing

Counselling room, school health room (UKS) and a healthy canteen.

Everyday convenience

School shop, parking area, clean restrooms and Wi-Fi.

Learning hall

A hall for assemblies, parent meetings and school events.

Transport and catering

School transport and catering are available through the school cooperative. Families register directly with the cooperative, which also runs a student savings programme.

Online services

The school runs its registration and academic systems online.

Online enrolment

Register from home through the enrolment form.

Academic system

Parents and students can follow academic information online.

Online payment

School payments can be made through the school’s payment system.
